## SLIDE 2 ù PASSENGER NEEDS & OPPORTUNITY



### Main heading



**PASSENGER NEEDS & OPPORTUNITY**



### Opening statement



Railway journeys involve multiple decisions before travel, during booking, and when unexpected issues occur. RailSahayak explores whether clearer, structured guidance can help passengers identify appropriate next steps.



### Section 01 ù BEFORE TRAVEL



**Route and Reservation Guidance**



Passengers may need help understanding station codes, route options, quota terminology, reservation status, and official information sources.



### Section 02 ù WHEN A PAYMENT FAILS



**Understand the Next Step**



A payment debit without a generated ticket can leave a passenger uncertain about transaction status, refund procedures, and whether to contact the bank or booking platform.



### Section 03 ù RESERVATION & JOURNEY STATUS



**Understand the Applicable Rules**



Passengers may need clear explanations of waitlist status, charting, ticket category, and the limits of the information shown by an independent advisory tool.



### Section 04 ù AFTER AN INCIDENT



**Find the Appropriate Support Channel**



Passengers may need assistance identifying the relevant official channel for a commercial, payment, operational, or safety-related concern.



### Closing statement



**Opportunity:** Make procedural guidance easier to understand without replacing official railway systems.

## SLIDE 3 ù PROBLEM STATEMENT



### Main heading



**THE PROBLEM STATEMENT**



### Main paragraph



Passengers can face uncertainty when the next step depends on the type of ticket, transaction state, railway rule, or responsible service channel. The proposed focus of RailSahayak is to organize these questions into clearer guidance and structured next steps.



### Problem 01 ù TRANSACTION UNCERTAINTY



A payment debit without ticket generation can create uncertainty about transaction reconciliation, refund procedures, and the appropriate support channel.



### Problem 02 ù RULE COMPLEXITY



Reservation status and ticket category can affect what a passenger should do next. Explanations must be accurate, current, and tied to the relevant rules.



### Problem 03 ù SUPPORT-CHANNEL CONFUSION



Commercial, payment, operational, and safety issues may require different official channels. Clear routing guidance can help passengers understand where to seek authoritative assistance.

## SLIDE 4 ù OUR SOLUTION



### Main heading



**OUR SOLUTION**



### Introductory paragraph



RailSahayak is an independent advisory prototype that brings selected passenger-support workflows into one interface. It aims to explain possible next steps and point users toward official channels when authoritative action or confirmation is required.



### Step 01 ù CLARIFY



**Plain-Language Guidance**



Explain selected reservation, payment-help, and complaint procedures in clear language based on the functionality actually implemented.



### Step 02 ù ORGANIZE



**Structured Issue Capture**



Help organize relevant incident details and complaint information without requesting unnecessary payment secrets or sensitive credentials.



### Step 03 ù DIRECT



**Appropriate Next Steps**



Guide the passenger toward the relevant official service or support channel when an issue requires authoritative information or action.



### Important boundary



RailSahayak does not replace official booking systems. Live railway data and institutional integrations must not be represented as available unless verified and authorized.

### Comparison 01 ù OFFICIAL BOOKING SYSTEMS



Official systems handle the booking and transaction functions they are authorized to provide.



RailSahayak's proposed complementary role: explain selected procedures and guide users toward the relevant official process.



### Comparison 02 ù OFFICIAL TRAIN INFORMATION



Official sources remain authoritative for live train running information and other operational updates.



RailSahayak's proposed complementary role: explain where and how to check the relevant information without falsely claiming live access.



### Comparison 03 ù OFFICIAL GRIEVANCE CHANNELS



Official grievance services remain responsible for their established reporting and resolution processes.



RailSahayak's proposed complementary role: organize selected issue details and explain the next appropriate channel.
